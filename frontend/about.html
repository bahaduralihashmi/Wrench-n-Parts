About Wrench n Parts

Wrench n Parts is a complete marketplace for automobile spare parts, connecting verified sellers, workshops, and customers across the region.

Our mission

Make it easy to find the exact auto part you need, from a seller you can trust, and to find a workshop that can install it — all in one place.

What we offer

  • Browse and search thousands of auto parts across categories
  • Wishlist, cart, secure checkout, and order tracking
  • Verified workshops with appointment booking
  • MechBot, our 24/7 automotive assistant powered by AI + knowledge base
  • Multi-role accounts: customer, shopkeeper, workshop, admin
